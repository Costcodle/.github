## Costcodle

[Costcodle](https://costcodle.org) is a free daily Costco price guessing game. Every day there is one warehouse item and six guesses to land within 5% of its price. After each guess the receipt says go higher or go lower, and flags you as close once you are within 25%. A new item arrives at 00:00 UTC, with no account needed.

**Play**

- [Today's Costcodle](https://costcodle.org)
- [Costcodle Unlimited](https://costcodle.org/unlimited): endless practice rounds with past items, separate from the daily streak

**Open source**

[costcodle-rules](https://github.com/Costcodle/costcodle-rules) is the rules engine behind the game: guess judging, price parsing, the UTC daily schedule and the spoiler-free share text. Pure TypeScript with no dependencies, MIT licensed, also on [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/costcodle-rules).
